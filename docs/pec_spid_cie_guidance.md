# PEC, SPID, and CIE Guidance

UfficioFacile explains PEC, SPID, and CIE locally through term pages and procedure guidance.

## PEC

- explain what PEC is and why it matters
- explain that a PEC-style message only has formal PEC value when sent from a PEC account when required
- show provider examples without endorsing one provider as best
- never show fake prices or stale pricing claims

## SPID and CIE

- explain that public portals may require one of them
- tell the user to use official identity and portal flows
- avoid claiming that one credential is always required unless the procedure guidance says so clearly
