# Official Contacts Policy

UfficioFacile must not invent official contacts.

## Rules

- do not create fake official emails or PEC addresses
- do not mark unverified records as verified
- do not show an exact address as verified without a clear source
- when exact data is missing, show guidance on how to find the correct office or provider contact
- always warn the user to verify the contact or page before sending personal data

## Local-first safety

The app stores guidance locally and can work without login or Supabase. Future remote directories must still follow the same verification rules.
