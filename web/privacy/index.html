Purpose of the app

UfficioFacile helps users navigate Italian bureaucracy and organize practical next steps across categories such as ASL, rent, utilities, telecom, university, INPS or patronato matters, public offices, bonuses, benefits, loans, credit, canone RAI, and general administrative issues.

Important disclaimer

UfficioFacile is not a public authority, government portal, lawyer, accountant, CAF, patronato, immigration consultant, or official representative. The app provides practical guidance and organization support only. It does not replace legal, medical, tax, accounting, or official government advice.

Data we may process

  • Email address, account state, and account identifiers.
  • Profile information entered by the user, including full name, city, preferred language, and codice fiscale if entered.
  • Saved requests, problem descriptions, consultancy or support requests, reminder preferences, and related workflow details.
  • Premium entitlement status, checkout status, subscription state, or payment status metadata.
  • Technical data, reliability logs, or diagnostics where applicable.

Payments

UfficioFacile may use Stripe for premium checkout and payment-related status updates. UfficioFacile does not store payment card numbers directly. Stripe handles payment card details.

Service providers and processors

  • Supabase for authentication, database storage, backend data, and related services.
  • Stripe for checkout, subscription, and payment status handling.
  • Vercel or related hosting for public web and admin-hosted surfaces where applicable.
  • Google AdMob only if ads are enabled in the production build.

Ads

The current intended first Google Play internal-testing release keeps ads disabled unless real production AdMob IDs are configured. If ads are enabled later, the AdMob-related section applies and Play declarations must be updated to match the final build.

Account deletion and local data deletion

Users can delete their account inside the app through Profile → Account and privacy → Delete my account, then confirm by typing DELETE.

If in-app deletion is unavailable or fails, users can still request deletion by email from Profile → Account and privacy → Request deletion by email or directly at support@ufficiofacile.app.

Deletion is intended to cover the account and associated app data where legally possible. Local app data is cleared and the user is signed out after successful self-service deletion.

Some payment, invoice, fraud-prevention, security, or legally required records may still be retained or detached from the deleted account where appropriate.

More details are available on the public account deletion page.

User rights

Depending on applicable law, including GDPR-oriented privacy frameworks, users may request access, correction, deletion, or other privacy-related assistance by contacting support@ufficiofacile.app.

Retention and deletion

Data may be retained as needed to provide the app, support user requests, maintain billing or entitlement records, comply with legal obligations, or protect the service. Payment, invoice, tax, and other legally required records may need to be retained even after account deletion.

Security

UfficioFacile is intended to use encrypted transport for network communication. Account deletion is performed server-side through an authenticated Supabase Edge Function. Client applications must not contain service-role keys.
