UfficioFacile account deletion

This page explains how to delete your UfficioFacile account and associated app data where legally possible.

Delete your account in the app

Open Profile → Account and privacy → Delete my account.

The app requires you to type DELETE before the final deletion button is enabled.

What happens after successful deletion

  • Your account is deleted from the app backend.
  • Associated app data is deleted where legally possible.
  • Local app data is cleared.
  • You are signed out of the app.

What may still remain

Some payment, invoice, fraud-prevention, security, or legally required records may still be retained or detached from the deleted account where required.

Email fallback

If in-app deletion is unavailable or fails, you can still request deletion by email from Profile → Account and privacy → Request deletion by email or by writing directly to: support@ufficiofacile.app

Suggested subject:

UfficioFacile account deletion request

Suggested body:

Account email:
Full name:
Request:
Please delete my UfficioFacile account and associated app data where legally possible.

Local-only device data

The app also keeps a separate Delete local data action inside Profile → Account and privacy. The privacy center remains available from the same section for sync and local-data controls, but full account deletion is no longer hidden there.
