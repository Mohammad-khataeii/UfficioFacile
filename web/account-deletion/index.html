UfficioFacile account deletion support

This page explains how to request deletion of your UfficioFacile account and associated app data where legally possible.

Request deletion from inside the app

Open Account → Privacy Center → Request account deletion. This opens a support email template.

Request deletion by email

Email: support@ufficiofacile.app

Suggested subject:

UfficioFacile account deletion request

Suggested body:

Account email:
Full name:
Request:
Please delete my UfficioFacile account and associated app data where legally possible.

Important note

This is a support-based deletion process. It is not an instant self-service deletion flow inside the client app.

Local device data

Local-only device data can be cleared separately through the privacy center in the app. Local data deletion is separate from the account deletion request.

Legal retention

Some data may need to be retained for legal, billing, accounting, fraud-prevention, or security reasons, including payment or invoice records where applicable.
